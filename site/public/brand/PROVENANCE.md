# Walt and the W mark

Walt is the Waggle bee: a compact apricot bee with ink stripes, dot eyes, a small smile, two antennae, and soft wings. The vector is a normalized drawing, not a pixel trace of the rasters.

The mark is a W inside brackets. The brackets are a claimed work boundary. The W is a waggle run. Stroke colors are `#181B1E` on light surfaces and `#ECEFF1` on dark. The palette is in `tokens/waggle-tokens.css`: apricot `#FFA161`, blue `#506AFD`, paper `#F5F5F4`.
