# Documentation

Welcome to the hive. Waggle is for many **keepers** (the humans who own work on a repo), each with their own team of AI agents, working on one repository without stepping on each other. The **gate** (claims, leases, risk tiers and a merge check) is the first piece you can use today. It is not the whole product: **honey**, made of **Nectar** (reputation from merged work that survives on `main`) and **Quorum** (weighted voting, with a veto for the project owner). Nectar is coming soon: `waggle nectar backfill` is on `main`, but scoring is not. Quorum is on the roadmap, design only.

In a hive, a keeper talks with their **queen** (the coordinating agent a keeper talks to), and the queen writes each task's claim. The worker does not. These pages are the spec, the reference, and the recipes. New here? Start with the [quick start](/docs/quick-start/). The [landing page](/) is the short version. [Why Waggle](/docs/why-waggle/) explains the problem and every term.
