## Must-fix

**M1. Section 2.1 lets a claim entry reach gate output, and sections 2.4, 5 and AC14 forbid that.**
Section 2.1 says "Error text may quote an entry only through `%q`", and `validateLeaseFields` will call `ValidateBranches`. On main, a stored claim that fails `validateLeaseFields` carries that error text as the malformed reason: `decodeLease` returns `err.Error()` (`internal/comb/git.go:477-479`), `ReadClaim` wraps it as `"%s: %w: %s", ref, ErrMalformedClaim, c.reason` (`internal/comb/claim_read.go:37`), and the gate prints it as `id+": "+err.Error()` (`cmd/waggle/gate_commits.go:321-322`). AC3 exercises exactly this path ("a forged claim ref with `"branches":["a..b"]` reads as malformed and `claimFor` fails it"). A builder who quotes the entry with `%q`, as 2.1 allows, therefore puts a claim entry into a gate message. Section 2.4 says messages carry "never the head name, the repo names or a claim entry". Section 5 lists "a claim entry echoed in a gate message" as a must-fix, and AC14 (review) checks it. Writing the claim is L3, so this is not an attack, but the brief contradicts itself on a must-fix line. One of the two guards will flag the build whichever way the builder goes.
Fix, either one: (a) preferred: `ValidateBranches` errors name the entry by position and rule, never by its bytes (for example `branches entry 2: not a valid branch name: comb: invalid lease`). Section 2.1 then drops the `%q` sentence and AC3 adds "the error does not contain the entry". (b) Keep `%q` and carve it out in 2.4, section 5 and AC14 as "except the existing malformed-claim reason, quoted with `%q` and passed through `annotData`, as directory and keeper errors already are". (a) costs nothing and keeps the must-fix list absolute.
