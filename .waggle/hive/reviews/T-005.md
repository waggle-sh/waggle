Blast radius: internal/comb only, no change to `Backend`. The check is advisory against anyone with raw push access (D-009). The deviation from section 7 (root commit) and the wildcard audit fetch need Head Keeper acknowledgement. Head Keeper approval required for merge (Tier 3 by content).
