## Intent Packet

Adds `internal/config`: a stdlib-only YAML subset parser (block mappings, plain and quoted scalars, fail-closed on lists, anchors, tags, block scalars, documents, duplicates, tabs) and a typed mapper that validates `version`, `forge` and `leases` against the 3.2 bounds. `Config.CombOptions(repoDir)` maps remote, comb mode, TTL and max directories into `comb.Options`; unknown top-level sections (including `waggle:`) are parsed and ignored. FuzzParse and table tests cover rejections and the live template.
Blast radius: new package only, nothing imports it yet. Future CLI and `waggled` wiring will inherit its strictness (unknown forge or leases keys now fail). Keeper approval required for merge.
