## 1. Goal

`MergedPull.Approvers` (`MergedPRByHead`, `MergedPRByCommit`) used the commit GitHub reports for a review, the value T-120 stopped trusting in the gate. Read each candidate's body and keep only logins whose body names the merged head on a `waggle-approve` line. A failed body read is an error, as a failed reviews read already is.

## 2. Checks

`TestMergedPRByHeadApprovers`, `TestMergedPRByHeadApproverNamesHead`, `TestMergedPRByCommit`; `go test -race ./...`.

## 3. Decision needed

`approvers` drives Nectar's self-merge factor (0.5 in a multi-Keeper repo, `internal/nectar/score.go`) and the reciprocity discount (`internal/nectar/gaming.go`). Requiring the line means a reviewer who clicks Approve without it, the normal case off protected paths, leaves `approvers: []`, and the unit scores as a self-merge. Merged PRs from before the line do the same on backfill. Keeping the reported commit lets an approval of an earlier head count as review of the merged head. Options: require the line everywhere; require it but score an empty list from a plain-Approve review as reviewed; or leave receipts as they are and close T-121.
