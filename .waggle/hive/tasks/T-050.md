## 1. Goal

Define Nectar as VISION.md core item 5 states it: reputation from merged commits that survive 30 days on `main` without a revert. The gate's claim and merge records are the input (D-232: the lease store stays claim refs).

## 2. Scope

- Scoring rules: what counts as a merged commit, how the 30-day survival window is measured, and how a revert claws back credit.
- Attribution: from claim owners (`keeper_id`) on the claim refs the gate already reads.
- Gameability and prior-art overlap (Wasteland stamps, Gittensor, per repositioning.md section 4) as design risks to answer, not grounds to delete the feature (D-366).
