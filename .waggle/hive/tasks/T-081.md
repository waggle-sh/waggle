## 1. Goal

Plain `waggle status` syncs claim refs twice in remote mode: once in `ListActiveLeases` and again in `CaseCollisions`. That costs a second fetch, and the collision lines can come from a newer snapshot than the claims table above them. Read both from one sync.

## 2. Scope

- A comb reader that returns active leases and case collisions from one scan, or a way to scan without a second sync.
- `waggle status` uses it, so one run fetches once and the table and the warnings agree.
- Tests that count the fetches and check the table and the warnings come from one snapshot.

## 3. Out of scope

- Changing the overlap rule or `DirsOverlap`.
- `waggle hives status` (T-084).
