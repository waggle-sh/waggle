# T-143: Reprioritization check: flag an agent reprioritizing its own claimed issue

- **Status:** Proposed under D-410 and D-411 on 2026-11-26 by the T-124 claims-first brief, governance scope from the Head Keeper's ruling (item 55, e1759). Stub only: the scout writes the target, stop and planned cut before any dispatch. Not claimed, not dispatched.
- **Release:** claims first.
- **Kind:** product. **Size:** S.
- **Layer:** `product`. **Hive effect at merge:** none until a Head Keeper pin move.
- **Gate:** `full`.
- **Risk Tier:** Tier 3 by content: it applies who may reprioritize and writes issue comments (D-205).
- **Role:** `scout` (brief), `builder` (dispatch), `guard` (review).
- **Lease (rw):** set by the scout; expected `internal/claims/reprioritize*.go` (new), one call in `cmd/waggle/sweep*.go`.
- **Depends on:** T-132, T-141 and T-142 merged; Q-T124-12 ruled.
- **Commit subject prefix:** `T-143:`

## 1. Goal

On each sweep, compare each actively claimed issue's priority with the `priority:` recorded in its claim and its change events; flag a change by the claim's holder that `MayReprioritize` rejects as a violation, and an unattributable change as unattributed, with an issue comment, a line in `waggle claims` and the sweep summary.

## 2. Scope

- Never reverts a change; an owner does.
- One flag comment per change, edited, not repeated.
- Attribution rules per Q-T124-12.

## 3. Out of scope

- Enforcement in the gate. Reprioritization by people who hold no claim.

## 4. Origin

T-124 sections 8 and 11 (claims-first refocus, D-410 and D-411, governance ruling item 55, e1759).
