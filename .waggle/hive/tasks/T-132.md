## 1. Goal

`waggle sweep` marks expired claims `state: expired` and clears the label and assignee it set; `waggle heartbeat --every <d>` renews until stopped; `waggle dispatch` renews issue claims as it renews refs.

## 2. Scope

- Server time plus grace; never touches an unparseable comment.
- Flags claims older than `claims.max_age`.

## 3. Out of scope

- Automatic release of old but live claims.
