## 1. Goal

`waggle sweep`, run every 15 minutes from a timer on our runner host, repairs everything the CLI left undone.

## 2. Scope

- Writes only new-kind claim refs: never deletes, releases or takes over a `ticket-<n>` ref; for hive claims it only renders windows and reports.
- Its memory between runs is `refs/waggle/sweep-state` (last run time, claim oids seen), written only by the sweep with an expected old value; missing or damaged state means a 24-hour lookback and no vanished notices that run.
- Delete claims past expiry plus grace; release claims whose PR merged (`Waggle-Task:` trailer or `Closes #<n>`); every deletion logged.
- Re-render every mirrored issue and window from refs and files; open window issues for hive claims.
- The "claim vanished" notice; reports for holders over cap, over-long leases and PRs from a lead that did not hold the task.
- Planned cut: the reports follow the first four steps.

## 3. Out of scope

- A GitHub-hosted scheduled workflow. The reprioritization check (T-143).
