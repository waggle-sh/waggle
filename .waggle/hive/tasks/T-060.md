## 1. Goal

A solo or early project adopts Waggle on day one with no friction and later flips one config line to enforce it, with no history backfill, because claims are recorded from the first task. Design: `docs/rfc-notes/staged-gate-modes.md`.

## 2. Scope

- `gate.mode: open | watch | guard` in `.waggle/control/config.yaml`. The check always runs; only consequences change.
- `open`: record, report would-be violations, always pass. `watch`: same, plus a PR comment. `guard`: today's v0.1 behavior with the check required.
- CLI: `waggle gate guard` (PR plus ruleset), `waggle gate open` (Head Keeper approval, no quiet unlocking), `waggle gate status` (would-have-blocked over 30 days).
- Default `guard` for existing v0.1 hives. `waggle init` picks by explicit answer and uses `guard` when it cannot ask.
- Plain names only: open, watched and guarded hive.

## 3. Out of scope

- Any Go code, workflow or scaffold change. Code slices are cut after the decision (RFC section 8).
- New modes, per-PR modes, new role or mascot names.
- Lowering the Tier 3 status of `.waggle/control/config.yaml`.

## 4. Acceptance

- The RFC note covers the modes, the no-backfill argument, the CLI, new-install defaults with a recommendation, migration for v0.1 users, security (base-config read, path tiering, who may loosen), naming and open questions.
- No em or en dashes, SPDX headers present, no private names or paths.
- The guard confirms nothing in the RFC lowers an existing v0.1 protection in `guard` mode.
