## 1. Goal

## 2. Scope

- `waggle telemetry send`: print the payload, then send it; refuse when `enabled` is false.
- A scaffolded weekly workflow that reads the last 7 days of gate counts artifacts, sends at a random minute in a 24 hour window, and writes the payload to the job summary.
- Only official endpoint by default; no retries that could duplicate a week.
