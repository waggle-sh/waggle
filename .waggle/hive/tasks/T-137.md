## 1. Goal

With `gate.claims_source: issue`, the gate finds the PR's issue (`Closes #N`, branch, or `Waggle-Issue:` trailer), reads its active claim and checks changed paths against the claim's paths, report-only by default. It also reports a PR that touches `governance.steering_paths` without an approval from `governance.owners` at its exact head, reusing the protected-path approver check.

## 2. Scope

- Default off; today's T-id checks unchanged.
- Steering-path check reads owners from T-141's config; today's `gate.approvers` still applies where set.
- Read-only token; runs from the Action's pinned tree.

## 3. Out of scope
