## 1. Goal

T-103 RFC section 7, item 11: post-merge audit and breach record. Design: `docs/rfc-notes/telemetry-insights-held-hive.md`.

## 2. Scope

- A workflow on push to the default branch, plus a daily sweep, compares first-parent commits with `refs/waggle/audit/merges`.
- Breaches go to `refs/waggle/audit/breaches` with a `verify` re-run at that head.
- `on_breach: issue` by default (open question 8); `revert` opens a revert PR that itself goes through `waggle merge`; the audit never merges.
- Clean ungated merges are recorded and reported weekly only.

## 3. Out of scope

- Preventing manual merges (not possible on these plans).
